Grade scale & calculation

A: 90 or higher · B+: 85 to below 90 · B: 80 to below 85 · C+: 75 to below 80 · C: 70 to below 75 · D+: 65 to below 70 · D: 60 to below 65 · F: below 60.

Course grade = homework × .15 + Perusall × .10 + readings × .05 + AI fluency × .05 + min(100, (three exam scores after any optional-final replacement + bonus) ÷ 3) × .65.

The summary shows two decimals for readability; the calculated percentage appears in this section. Letter grades use the unrounded value. For example, 89.99% is a B+, not an A. Any rounding of official final grades is at your instructor’s discretion. The exam average, including bonus points, is capped at 100%; the exam category contributes at most 65 percentage points. The optional final replaces one lowest exam only when higher, then bonus points are added once and the average is calculated. Individual exam scores are combined before this category cap is applied.
